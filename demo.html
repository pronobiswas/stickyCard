Planning to start a (new) business

The problem

Starting something new can feel overwhelming, even if you’ve done business before. Common struggles include:

  • juggling multiple ideas, tasks, and deadlines
  • uncertainty about the next step to take
  • fear of missing something important
  • spending time on “busy work” instead of real progress
  • constantly shifting plans and changing priorities
  • no clear system to organize your ideas and goals
Solopreneur

The problem

Running a business on your own often means:

  • wasting time searching for information and maintaining inefficient records
  • juggling project deadlines, invoicing, payments, and both your own and your clients’ obligations
  • too many alarms, reminders, and calendars, and yet still missing things
  • unclear visibility into finances and business performance
  • working in a system that isn’t sustainable long-term
Managing a small team (up to 5 employees)

The problem

As a small team grows, new challenges often emerge:

  • Important information is scattered across emails, chats, and various communication tools
  • It’s difficult to know which document version is the most up-to-date
  • Tasks and responsibilities are not always clearly defined
  • Project communication happens across multiple channels, with status and phase often unclear
